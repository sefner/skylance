How to play
  1. 1Hold and drag across enemies and incoming shots to lock on. Tough targets take a second pass.
  2. 2Release to loose homing lances. Each kill scores × the locks in that volley.
  3. 3Turn with ◀ ▶ or a two-finger swipe when the radar shows threats beside or behind you.
  4. 4Berserk when the gold meter fills: every target in range, all at once.
  5. 5Charge: keep holding after you lock on. Each level adds lance damage, but enemies keep firing while you wait.
  6. 6Colours: a volley that kills three or more of one eye colour pays double.
  7. 7Grow: kills feed your dragon. It evolves from Hatchling to Elder, and every evolution adds locks and a heart.
